## Winter Is Coming ❄️

**Tech4Change Winter Edition 2026** is a special winter edition of the
Tech4Change project innovation contest, organised as a sister event of
the **7th CINE Conference** and inspired by the vision of
**Viksit Bharat 2047**.

Building on the success of Tech4Change 2026, the Winter Edition invites
B.Tech students, student innovators, developers, and early-career
researchers to turn meaningful ideas into practical technology-driven
solutions for real-world challenges.

This edition carries a distinctive challenge-driven spirit:

> **Winter is Coming — Are You Ready to Build the Future?**

Participants are encouraged to identify pressing problems across
society, technology, sustainability, healthcare, education, governance,
industry, and other domains, and develop innovative, scalable, and
demonstrable prototypes that can create measurable impact.

### 🔥 What makes the Winter Edition different?

- Idea → Prototype → Impact
- Focus on working and demonstrable solutions, not just concepts
- Encourages AI/ML, IoT, Robotics, Software, Hardware, Data Science,
  and emerging technologies
- Emphasis on innovation, feasibility, scalability, and societal impact
- Open to interdisciplinary student teams
- A staged evaluation process, from concept note to prototype
  demonstration

### ❄️ Theme

**WINTER IS COMING**

The theme represents a challenge to innovators: anticipate tomorrow's
problems today and build solutions before the future arrives.
