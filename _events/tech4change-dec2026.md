## Winter Is Coming ❄️
**Tech4Change Winter Edition 2026** is a special winter edition of the
Tech4Change project innovation contest, organised as a sister event of
the **7th CINE Conference** and inspired by the vision of
**Viksit Bharat 2047**.
Building on the success of Tech4Change 2026, the Winter Edition invites
B.Tech students, student innovators, developers, and early-career
researchers to turn meaningful ideas into practical technology-driven
solutions for real-world challenges.
This edition carries a distinctive challenge-driven spirit:
> **Winter is Coming — Are You Ready to Build the Future?**
Participants are encouraged to identify pressing problems across
society, technology, sustainability, healthcare, education, governance,
industry, and other domains, and develop innovative, scalable, and
demonstrable prototypes that can create measurable impact.
### 🔥 What makes the Winter Edition different?
- Idea → Prototype → Impact
- Focus on working and demonstrable solutions, not just concepts
- Encourages AI/ML, IoT, Robotics, Software, Hardware, Data Science,
  and emerging technologies
- Emphasis on innovation, feasibility, scalability, and societal impact
- Open to interdisciplinary student teams
- A staged evaluation process, from concept note to prototype
  demonstration

### ❄️ Theme
**WINTER IS COMING**

The theme represents a challenge to innovators: anticipate tomorrow's
problems today and build solutions before the future arrives.

Participants can choose to work within any of the following frontier
technology tracks:

#### 🚀 SpaceTech
Build solutions inspired by or applicable to space exploration,
satellite systems, remote sensing, ground station communication, or
space-based data services.

*Sub-categories:*
- Satellite communication & telemetry systems
- Remote sensing & earth observation analytics
- Orbital mechanics simulation & mission planning tools
- Space debris tracking & collision avoidance models
- Low-cost CubeSat / nanosatellite prototypes

#### 🤖 Autonomous Agents & Intelligent Systems
Design agents, bots, or multi-agent systems that can perceive,
reason, plan, and act with minimal human intervention — targeting
real-world or simulated environments.

*Sub-categories:*
- Autonomous ground/aerial/underwater vehicles (physical or simulated)
- AI-powered decision-making & planning agents
- Multi-agent coordination and swarm intelligence
- LLM-based task automation & agentic workflows
- Human-robot interaction and adaptive control systems

#### 🌐 Emerging & Interdisciplinary Tech
Projects at the intersection of cutting-edge technologies with
social, environmental, or industrial challenges.

*Sub-categories:*
- Edge AI & TinyML for resource-constrained environments
- Cyber-physical systems & smart infrastructure
- Digital twins and simulation-based solutions
- Quantum computing concepts applied to real-world problems
- Blockchain/decentralised systems for governance or supply chains

---
> 💡 **Student-Friendly Approach:** All tracks are fully accessible
> using open-source tools, simulation platforms (e.g. Gazebo, AirSim,
> OpenMCT, Gymnasium), and low-cost hardware. Projects built entirely
> in simulation or on a shoestring budget are welcome and will be
> evaluated on their merit — though a **small, working physical
> prototype** will always be appreciated and may strengthen your
> evaluation.
