He is a Full-Stack Developer at SAP Labs India and is currently pursuing an M.Tech. at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras. His research interests include artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, speech processing, and brain–computer interfaces. He has authored publications in reputed international journals and conferences, including the IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics, where his work on real-time speech emotion recognition for smart home assistants received recognition at the 2023 IEEE International Conference on Consumer Electronics. 
Alongside his research, he actively develops scalable software solutions and contributes to open-source projects.
