Jheelam Mondal (Member, IEEE) received the B.Tech. and M.Tech. degrees in Computer Science and Engineering. She is pursuing her Ph.D. program at KIIT Deemed to be 
University, India. She is currently working as an Assistant Professor with the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Haldia Institute of Technology, 
Haldia, West Bengal, India. Her research areas include machine learning, deep learning and image processing.
